Why this blog exists

This is a personal record of solo travel — the routes, the detours, and the places that didn't make it onto a map until someone walked, rode, or paddled through them. Every pin on the globe is a real trip, told as honestly as I can manage.

New stories get added as new trips happen. Spin the globe and go exploring.
